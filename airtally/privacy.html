Privacy policy · 31 October 2026

Your logbook stays yours.

AirTally is developed by Matteo Benvenuti. You do not need an AirTally account to use the app, and the developer does not receive your logbook.

Data on your device

Flights, simulator sessions, aircraft, paper pages, page images, page figures, checks and revision history are stored on your device in the app’s own database, with system file protection. Entry, calculations, search, reports and backups work offline.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your records and page images are mirrored to your private iCloud database, and your display and entry preferences to your private iCloud key-value store, so your devices stay together. A small list of which of your devices have sync turned on is kept there too. These are stored in your iCloud account under Apple’s terms; the developer has no access to them.

Camera, photos and files

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a logbook page. Photos are chosen through the system picker without access to your library. Files you import are read only when you select them, and exports are saved only where you choose.

Page recognition

Scanned pages are recognised entirely on your device with Apple’s Vision framework. Page images and recognised text are not sent to the developer or to any external recognition or AI service.

Advertising, analytics and network use

AirTally has no server of its own, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDK and no advertising-identifier collection. Apart from iCloud when you enable it, the app makes no network requests. It does not sell personal information.

Support messages

If you email support, the email address, message and attachments you send are used to investigate and respond. They are processed by the email provider and retained as needed to resolve the issue and maintain support records. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any record in the app, export a complete backup, and turn iCloud sync off at any time; turning it off keeps your local logbook. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; records already in your private iCloud database remain there until you delete them through your iCloud settings. The developer cannot access or erase your iCloud data.

This website

These support pages contain no forms, advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting service processes ordinary network requests and may retain operational and security logs.

Contact and updates

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to matteo.benvenuti3399@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when the app’s behaviour changes; the date above identifies the current revision.
