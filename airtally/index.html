Getting started

  1. Add your aircraft under Aircraft, or let AirTally offer them when you enter a new registration.
  2. Create a paper logbook under Paper and add its pages, so flights can be placed on the page where you wrote them.
  3. Enter flights by hand, scan a page, or import a CSV file. Nothing counts until you review and commit it.

Everything works offline and without an account. iCloud sync is optional and can be turned on in Settings on each device.

A little help

Should I type 1:30 or 1.5?

Both mean one hour thirty minutes. Note that 1.30 in decimal hours is 1 hour 18 minutes, not 1:30. Stored durations are never rounded; displays may round and mark rounded values with ≈.

A page check says the figures do not match

Open the page and compare the brought-forward, subtotal and carried-forward figures with the flights assigned to it, in book and page order. AirTally never changes your paper figures to force a match; a check returns to “needs recheck” whenever a contributing flight changes.

A scanned row was read incorrectly

Recognition runs on your device and handwriting accuracy is not guaranteed. Every scanned row is a suggestion: open it in the review screen and correct or add any column before committing. When AirTally corrects a registration, place or name to one already in your logbook, the row says so.

My other device does not show my flights

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on every device, signed in to the same iCloud account, and allow a few minutes. Settings → Sync status shows what each device is doing. Sync mirrors your records; it is not a backup.

How do I keep a backup?

Use Backup to export an .airtally archive with every record, page image, figure and check, and keep it outside the app, for example in Files or on a computer. PDF reports and CSV exports are not complete backups.

How do I report a problem?

Email matteo.benvenuti3399@gmail.com with the app version, device model and what you expected to happen. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or a full logbook backup unless asked.

AirTally does not decide legal currency or eligibility, does not certify entries and is not approved by any aviation authority, insurer, club or employer. Your official logbook remains your record.
