Cleared for Takeoff: Rivals

Privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026

Who runs this app

Cleared for Takeoff: Rivals is a personal game project operated by Matteo Benvenuti. For privacy questions or requests, contact the support email below.

Solo play and device storage

Solo races and the daily challenge run on your device. Your saved races, personal records, career totals, daily challenge streak, airline settings, tip and audio preferences are stored locally. The app does not send solo gameplay to the multiplayer service. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any device backups you manage.

Online play

Creating or joining a room sends your chosen airline name and three-letter code, a randomly generated session credential, room membership and game actions to the separate mobile multiplayer service. Use a fictional airline name. Other players in the same room can see your airline name, code, score and shared game activity. The server stores a one-way hash of the credential to protect your airline controls; keep the original device to rejoin.

Service security and hosting

The service processes network requests and uses a short-lived hashed IP value and request counts to limit abuse. The service is hosted through OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure, whose operational systems may process connection and security logs. Public support, privacy and invitation pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary connection and security data when those pages are visited. The room code in an invitation link stays in your browser and is not sent to that page’s server. We do not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics in the game, and we do not sell personal data.

Retention

Online rooms are kept only while a race needs them. A lobby or a race in progress expires 2 hours after its last activity, and a finished race 15 minutes after it ends. A lobby or finished race is deleted as soon as every player has left it, and no room is kept longer than 24 hours after it was created. Expired rooms become inaccessible at once; they and expired rate-limit records are removed when the service next runs its cleanup, which happens while it handles requests. The short rate-limit windows expire within approximately two minutes. Local saves remain on your device until replaced or removed.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. You may request access, correction or deletion of identifiable online data by email; include the room code and airline name if relevant, but never send your private session credential. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights and the right to contact a data-protection authority.

No account or sensitive permissions

No account, real name, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access is required to play. Online play requires an internet connection. Invitations and daily challenge results can be copied or shared using the device’s own sharing tools. No real-money purchases, advertising or cash prizes are offered.

Changes

If the app’s data handling changes, this policy and any relevant App Store disclosures will be updated. The date above identifies the current version.
