Cleared for Takeoff: Rivals

Help & support

Start with Alder Cove

Choose an airport and Play solo. Alder Cove gives you one gate and a gentle start. Bigger airports add more traffic and tighter timing.

Get an aircraft away

Select your aircraft, choose its destination, and wait for approval at the gate. Follow the next action to prepare it and taxi to a runway. You give the final takeoff clearance. Check the map for landing aircraft first.

Bring an aircraft in

Select an arriving aircraft and assign a landing runway. It needs a free gate before it can land. After landing, choose a gate and send it there. Arrivals earn points when they reach the gate.

Play with friends

Choose Play online, create an airline, and create a room. Share the six-character code. Your friends use Join with a code, then everyone marks themselves ready. The host starts the race. Everyone must use this mobile app; website rooms are separate.

Sound and saved games

The music and speaker buttons control music and effects separately. Silent mode also mutes game audio. Solo play saves automatically and pauses when you leave the app. Online races continue while you are away; return on the same device to rejoin.
