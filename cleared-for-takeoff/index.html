Cleared for Takeoff: Rivals

Help & support

Start with Alder Cove

Choose an airport and Play solo. Alder Cove gives you one gate and a gentle start. Bigger airports add more traffic and tighter timing. Before you start, you can change the difficulty and the number of AI rivals.

Daily challenge

Every day brings one race on the same airport for everyone. Win it to keep your streak going. You can try as often as you like, and share your result.

Get an aircraft away

Select your aircraft, choose its destination, and wait for approval at the gate. Follow the next action to prepare it and taxi to a runway. You give the final takeoff clearance. Check the map for landing aircraft first.

Bring an aircraft in

Select an arriving aircraft and assign a landing runway. It needs a free gate before it can land. After landing, choose a gate and send it there. Arrivals earn points when they reach the gate.

After the race

The Race tab shows the result and your race report: departures, arrivals and where penalty points came from. Career, on the game’s Help & support screen, keeps your totals on your device.

Play with friends

Choose Play online, create an airline, and create a room. Share the invitation: its link opens the game with the room code filled in. Friends can also type the six-character code under Join with a code. Everyone marks themselves ready, then the host starts the race. Afterwards the host can start a rematch in the same room. Everyone must use this mobile app; website rooms are separate.

Sound and saved games

The music and speaker buttons turn music and effects on or off, and Settings has a volume slider for each. Silent mode also mutes game audio. Light haptic taps mark the race start, scoring, warnings and the result; they follow the System Haptics setting on your device. Solo play saves automatically and pauses when you leave the app. Online races continue while you are away; return on the same device to rejoin.
