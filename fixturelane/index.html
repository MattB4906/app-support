Make it yours

  1. Open Your setup and choose your home base.
  2. Choose your competitions and put them in your preferred order.
  3. Select the streaming services you have. Each country remembers its own selection.

Supported home bases are the United States, France, Italy, Spain, Germany and England (UK). Competition choices and ordering apply across regions. Preferences can sync through iCloud when it is enabled on your devices.

A little help

A game or broadcaster is missing

Pull down on the schedule or tap the refresh arrow. Future broadcast details may not have been announced yet, and listings can change. An unconfirmed listing means FixtureLane does not have enough information to confirm access.

The game time looks wrong

Check your device’s date and time settings. Optional location access helps FixtureLane identify your local time zone. Location is not required to browse the schedule.

A streaming button opens a website

FixtureLane tries the service’s supported app links first. When the service does not accept a link, its website opens. Availability and playback depend on the service, your plan and your location.

What does a VPN city suggestion mean?

It identifies one possible viewing location supported by the listing or coverage estimate. It does not start a VPN, change your location, or guarantee playback. Services can restrict VPN use and apply additional checks.

My devices have different preferences

Use the same iCloud account and allow time for changes to sync. Service choices are saved separately for each country, so check that you selected the same home base. Each device remembers its own active country.

How do I report a problem?

Email matteo.benvenuti3399@gmail.com with the app version, device model, selected country, date and affected game. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords, payment information or precise location details.

FixtureLane is a viewing guide. It does not provide video streams or subscriptions. Live status and local coverage are estimates and may change.
