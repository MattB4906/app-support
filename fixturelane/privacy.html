Privacy policy · 26 October 2026

Your settings and your privacy.

FixtureLane is developed by Matteo Benvenuti. You do not need a FixtureLane account to use the app.

Preferences and iCloud

Your competition choices, their order and the services selected for each country are saved on your device. When iCloud is available, these preferences and the revision information needed to merge edits are stored in your private iCloud key-value store. FixtureLane’s developer does not receive access to that store.

The active home base, date, filters and location preference remain local to each device. The app does not ask for or store streaming-service passwords or payment details.

Optional location

With your permission, FixtureLane uses approximate foreground location to determine local game times and estimate viewing availability. It does not request background location. Coordinates and the derived location are kept in memory rather than written into schedule caches or synced preferences.

The operating system’s geocoding service may process coordinates to provide a city, postal code and time zone. For supported local coverage checks, the app sends a postal code to the relevant coverage service. It does not send your coordinates to the sports-listing and coverage sites.

You can disable location in Your setup or in your device’s privacy settings. The schedule remains available using your device’s time zone and selected home base; some local coverage checks will be unavailable.

Listing requests and external services

FixtureLane retrieves schedules and viewing information from external websites and network services. Requests identify the requested competition, event or date and include standard connection information such as your IP address. Postal-code lookups additionally include the requested postal code.

Some listings are read through temporary embedded web sessions. Those pages can load scripts and resources operated by their providers. Embedded sessions use nonpersistent website storage, but external operators may keep network, security or access logs according to their own policies. FixtureLane’s developer does not control their retention practices.

Opening a viewing option takes you to another app or website. Its account, subscription, location and privacy rules apply there.

Advertising and analytics

FixtureLane has no advertising placements, analytics SDK or advertising-identifier collection of its own. It does not sell personal information. This does not prevent independent websites contacted by the app from processing the network requests described above.

Cached information

Schedules, programme listings and coverage data may be cached locally to improve refreshes and allow previously loaded information to remain visible. Personal coordinates and postal-code lookup results are not written into those caches. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; private iCloud preferences may remain in your iCloud account.

Support messages

If you email support, the email address, message and attachments you send are used to investigate and respond. They are processed by the email provider and retained as needed to resolve the issue and maintain support records. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Your choices

You can edit competition and service selections in the app, disable location access, and manage iCloud access through your device settings. The developer cannot access or erase your private iCloud preferences. To ask about support correspondence or other information you supplied directly, contact support.

This website

These support pages contain no forms, advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting service processes ordinary network requests and may retain operational and security logs.

Contact and updates

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to matteo.benvenuti3399@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when the app’s behaviour changes; the date above identifies the current revision.
